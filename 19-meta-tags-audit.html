Importance of Meta Tags

  • Charset: Defines the character encoding of the page.
  • Viewport: Helps pages display correctly on mobile devices.
  • Description: Provides a short summary of the webpage.
  • Title: Defines the title shown in the browser tab.
  • Author: Identifies the author of the webpage.
  • Robots: Gives instructions to search engine crawlers.

Conclusion
